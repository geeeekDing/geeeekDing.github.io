PROFILE

About me

I am an Associate Researcher in the School of Computer Science at Peking University. I received my PhD in Physics from Shandong University in 2021, supervised by Dian Min Tong, and was a postdoctoral researcher at PKU’s Center on Frontiers of Computing Studies from 2022 to 2025, working with Xiao Yuan.

My research spans quantum resource theory, quantum algorithms, and scientific computing. I study how to characterize quantum resources with limited or imperfect measurements, and develop methods for constrained quantum optimization and error mitigation, with applications to molecular docking, electronic structure, and quantum dynamics.

My work, including collaborations, has appeared in Nature Nanotechnology, Nature Communications, Science Advances, and Physical Review Letters, among other journals.

我目前在北京大学计算机学院任副研究员。2021年于山东大学获得理学博士学位，导师为仝殿民教授；2022—2025年在北京大学前沿计算研究中心从事博士后研究，合作导师为袁骁研究员。

我的研究围绕量子资源理论、量子算法与科学计算展开：一方面，研究如何利用有限且存在误差的测量来表征量子资源；另一方面，探索含约束的量子优化、错误缓解，以及量子计算在分子对接、电子结构和动力学模拟中的应用。

相关研究成果发表于 Nature Nanotechnology、Nature Communications、Science Advances、Physical Review Letters 等期刊。
